Pixi Components

Glass

.glass frosted card (blur on ::before, off under reduced transparency), with .glass-pill and .glass-circle shapes. Clickable glass lights up on hover; open (aria-expanded) is a solid disk.

Ghost button

.ghost-button: no border or fill at rest, a subtle fill on hover, a solid disk when pressed, active or open (inverted in the light theme), 35% when disabled. Circular for icons; add .ghost-text for a text pill.

Slider

.slider: a 6px track filled to the thumb, a white pill thumb, icons at both ends (the label is .visually-hidden), and a value bubble while adjusting. Set --f (0..1) and .is-adjusting from script.

Swatch pair

.swatch-pair: foreground and background as overlapping ringed circles in a 44px stack, with Swap and Reset as plain icons in the free corners.
