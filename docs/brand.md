# Forma — Brand Identity & Strategy

Forma is the company behind Pixi and Lines: fast, web-native graphics
tools. This is its positioning, company description, strategy, and voice.

> **AI is the direction, not yet a shipped feature.** Neither app has AI
> features today. In public copy (README, About, app UI), describe what the
> apps do now, and use the AI lines below only once an AI feature ships.
> Lines marked *(after AI ships)* are held until then.

## 1. Company description

### One-liner (tagline)

**Forma: Design primitives for the modern web.**

*(after AI ships)* Forma: AI-powered design primitives for the modern web.

### Short description (About Us / bio)

Forma is a web-native graphic software company crafting next-generation,
AI-assisted creative tools. We build lightweight, fast, and accessible design
software directly in the browser—eliminating the bloat of legacy graphic
suites. From pixel-level precision with Pixi to vector architecture with
Lines, Forma empowers creators to move seamlessly from concept to finished
asset.

### Long description (press / manifesto)

Creative software shouldn't feel like a heavy CAD system built in the 1990s.
At Forma, we are reimagining visual design tools ground-up for the web era.

We combine focused, minimal interfaces with smart, contextual AI—giving
creators power without complexity. Whether you're painting pixel art,
tweaking vector paths, or generating scalable assets, Forma tools run
instantly in your browser, keeping you in flow without subscriptions to
massive, overwhelming desktop suites.

Our core suite:

- **Pixi:** A nimble, web-native pixel editor for fast raster art and asset
  creation, with contextual AI assistance on the roadmap.
- **Lines:** A streamlined vector editor—Adobe Illustrator reduced to its
  sharpest, fastest essentials.

## 2. Brand strategy

### Core mission

To lower the barrier between idea and execution by providing lightweight,
AI-augmented design tools on the web.

### Position and competitive landscape

- **Legacy giants (Adobe / Corel):** High power, but bloated, expensive,
  desktop-bound, and steep learning curve.
- **Modern all-in-ones (Figma / Canva):** Great for UI or templates, but heavy
  for quick, focused asset creation.
- **Forma:** Focused, instant, web-native primitives. Built for creators who
  need specialized, lightning-fast canvas tools (pixels vs. vectors) enhanced
  with low-friction AI capabilities.

### Target audience

- **Indie game developers and pixel artists:** Needing fast asset generation
  and manual pixel polishing (Pixi).
- **Web designers and developers:** Needing quick SVG manipulation, icons, and
  vector paths without opening Illustrator (Lines).
- **Digital creators and marketers:** Wanting fast graphics tools right inside
  the browser tab.

## 3. Brand voice and tone

### Personality attributes

1. **Precise:** Grounded in geometry, vectors, and pixels. Clear and direct.
2. **Lightweight:** No jargon, no fluff, no unnecessary steps.
3. **Smart, not loud:** AI is treated as a subtle assistant, not a gimmick.
4. **Focused:** Respects the user's canvas and flow state.

### Copywriting guidelines

| Dimension | Do this | Avoid this |
|---|---|---|
| On AI features | "Describe your shape, tweak the path." | "Revolutionary magic magic AI algorithm!" |
| On performance | "Instant canvas. Loads in under a second." | "Enterprise-grade high-throughput render pipeline." |
| On UX | "Just the tools you need. Nothing in your way." | "Feature-packed with 500+ hidden submenus." |

  *(after AI ships)* "Pixel precision, AI speed."
